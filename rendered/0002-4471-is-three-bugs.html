Context

#4471 merged 412 reports under one subject and one status: known, one fix queued. Subject-matching dedupe (decision 0001) is fast, but it does not read the report. src/lib/kb/causes.ts already computes a real cause per report, and CauseTable already flags a second cause inside #4471 as "not this ticket — no ticket of its own." desk-notes.json already footnotes five reports as something else again. Nobody opened tickets for either.

One subject line, three bugs. #4471 as filed covers only the first.

Decision

Open one ticket per cause above, each carrying its own count, instead of leaving all three under #4471's single "known" status. #4471 stays as the record of the merge; the three new tickets carry the fixes.

Engineering's own update on #4471, closed 05 Oct

  • parser — fixed: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. Ticket A closed as completed.
  • hint — won't fix: the booking-v3 form was retired, so the mismatch no longer applies. Not a code fix; ticket B closed as not planned.
  • one-day move is not part of this update. It is a different symptom from both, still open as ticket C.

Consequences

  • Engineering can ship the hint fix (read the form the way its own hint says) without it hiding behind the parser fix (locale-aware parsing).
  • The five oneDayMove reports stop being miscounted as evidence for the month/day swap; they get looked at as their own, much smaller, problem.
  • #4471's "known. one issue, one fix queued" is no longer accurate and should be corrected to point at the three tickets.
